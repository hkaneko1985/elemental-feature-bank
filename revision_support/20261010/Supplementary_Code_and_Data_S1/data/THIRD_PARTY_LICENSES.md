## Release rule

- `GREEN`: numerical values may appear in the public matrices, subject to attribution and source-specific obligations.
- `AMBER`: numerical values are excluded until the exact asset/version and obligations are closed.
- `HOLD`: numerical values are excluded until explicit permission or an applicable asset-level licence is documented.

The detailed audit workbook is available at `docs/licensing/license_audit_elemental_feature_dataset.xlsx`.
